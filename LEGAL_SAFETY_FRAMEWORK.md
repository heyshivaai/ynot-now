## ⚠️ THE PROBLEM: HIGH-RISK FINDING IDENTIFIED

**Dangerous Title:**
> "Suspicious Pattern of Exact Automation Percentages Across Multiple Vendors Suggests Coordinated Marketing Rather Than Measured Results"

**Why This is a Legal Liability:**
1. ❌ **"Suspicious Pattern"** - Implies wrongdoing/fraud
2. ❌ **"Coordinated Marketing"** - Accuses vendors of collusion
3. ❌ **"Rather Than Measured Results"** - Calls claims false
4. ❌ **Targets multiple vendors** - Broader defamation exposure

**Legal Risks:**
- Defamation (false statements harming reputation)
- Trade libel (disparaging products/services)
- Tortious interference (harming business relationships)
- Potential damages: $50K-$500K+ per vendor

## ✅ SAFE REFRAMING - OBSERVE, DON'T ACCUSE

### **UNSAFE → SAFE Examples**

**EXAMPLE 1: The Dangerous Finding**

❌ **UNSAFE:**

✅ **SAFE:**
> "Multiple Vendors Report Similar Automation Rates Without Publishing Independent Validation Methodology"

## 📋 THE GOLDEN RULES (Non-Negotiable)

### **RULE 1: Observe, Don't Accuse**
- ✅ State what IS or ISN'T documented
- ❌ Never imply intent (fraud, lying, hiding)

### **RULE 2: Descriptive, Not Judgmental**
- ✅ "Claims lack independent validation"
- ❌ "Suspicious", "dubious", "questionable", "alleged"

### **RULE 3: Factual Gap, Not Character Flaw**
- ✅ "Methodology not disclosed"
- ❌ "Vendor refuses to disclose", "hiding details"

### **RULE 4: Present Data, Not Theories**
- ✅ "Three vendors report 85% automation"
- ❌ "Coordinated marketing", "collusion", "copying"

### **RULE 5: Educational Tone**
- ✅ "Readers should seek independent benchmarks"
- ❌ "Don't trust vendor claims", "be suspicious"

## 🚫 BANNED WORDS & PHRASES (Never Use)

### **Accusatory Language:**
- ❌ Suspicious, dubious, questionable, alleged
- ❌ Exposed, revealed, uncovered, caught
- ❌ Hype, washing, fake, fabricated, false
- ❌ Hiding, refusing, avoiding, evading
- ❌ Coordinated, collusion, conspiracy
- ❌ Misleading, deceptive, dishonest

### **Judgmental Phrases:**
- ❌ "Suggests [negative intent]"
- ❌ "Rather than [what's real]"
- ❌ "Despite claims of..."
- ❌ "In reality..."
- ❌ "The truth is..."

### **Investigative Journalist Tone:**
- ❌ "Our investigation found..."
- ❌ "We discovered..."
- ❌ "Evidence shows..."
- ❌ "Contrary to their statements..."

## ✅ SAFE VOCABULARY (Always Use)

### **Neutral Descriptors:**
- ✅ Reports, states, claims, announces
- ✅ Published, disclosed, documented
- ✅ Not published, not disclosed, not documented
- ✅ Independent validation, third-party verification
- ✅ Methodology, benchmarks, audit

### **Educational Framing:**
- ✅ "Readers should note..."
- ✅ "Independent verification not available"
- ✅ "External benchmarks show..."
- ✅ "Third-party validation would clarify..."

### **Factual Observations:**
- ✅ "Claims have not been independently verified"
- ✅ "Methodology not published"
- ✅ "Differs from industry benchmarks"
- ✅ "Technical details not disclosed"

## 📐 THE SAFE FRAMING FORMULA

```
[ENTITY] + [ACTION VERB] + [CLAIM/STAT] + [VERIFICATION STATUS]
```

**Examples:**

1. **"Company X reports 75% efficiency improvement; independent audit pending"**
   - Entity: Company X
   - Action: reports
   - Claim: 75% efficiency
   - Status: audit pending

   - Status: architecture not published

   - Entity: Study
   - Action: finds

## 📊 BEFORE/AFTER TRANSFORMATION

### **Finding 1: Automation Claims**

**BEFORE (RISKY):**
```
Title: Suspicious Pattern of Exact Automation Percentages Across Multiple 
       Vendors Suggests Coordinated Marketing Rather Than Measured Results
Body: Three major insurtech vendors—Company A, B, and C—all claim exactly 
      85% claims automation in Q4 2025, despite using different technology 
      stacks. This identical figure, combined with lack of independent audits, 
      suggests coordinated messaging rather than actual measured performance.
Verdict: UNVERIFIED
```

**AFTER (SAFE):**
```
Title: Multiple Vendors Report 85% Claims Automation; Independent Validation 
       Methodologies Not Published
Body: Three insurtech vendors reported 85% claims automation rates in Q4 2025. 
      The vendors use different technology architectures. Independent third-party 
      validation methodologies and audit reports have not been published. Industry 
      benchmark data for claims automation typically ranges from 45-75% based on 
      peer-reviewed studies.
Verdict: UNVERIFIED
Experiment: Commission independent benchmarking study comparing vendor-reported 
           automation rates with observed throughput metrics
```

**What changed:**
- ✅ Removed "Suspicious Pattern", "Coordinated Marketing"
- ✅ Added factual context (industry benchmarks)
- ✅ Neutral tone: "reported" not "claim"
- ✅ Focus on verification gap, not intent
- ✅ Constructive experiment suggestion

## 🔒 LEGAL SAFETY CHECKLIST (Use Before Publishing)

### **Pre-Publication Review:**

**1. Title Check:**
- [ ] No accusatory words (suspicious, dubious, exposed, etc.)
- [ ] No implied intent (coordinated, hiding, refusing, etc.)
- [ ] States facts, not judgments
- [ ] Could be read aloud to the vendor without embarrassment

**2. Body Check:**
- [ ] All claims attributed to sources
- [ ] Verification status stated factually
- [ ] No "suggests [negative]" constructions
- [ ] Industry context provided (benchmarks, norms)
- [ ] Neutral tone throughout

**3. Verdict Alignment:**
- [ ] UNVERIFIED = factual statement about verification
- [ ] Not used to imply "this is false"
- [ ] Not used to imply "this is hype"

**4. Experiment Check:**
- [ ] Constructive, not accusatory
- [ ] Suggests research, not investigation
- [ ] Educational value clear

**5. Legal Red Flags:**
- [ ] No company names + negative claims without proof
- [ ] No fraud/collusion implications
- [ ] No "refuses"/"hiding"/"avoiding" language
- [ ] No comparison to what's "really" happening

**6. Tone Test:**
- [ ] Sounds like university research brief, not tabloid exposé
- [ ] Vendor could respond: "Fair representation of facts"
- [ ] Not sensational or inflammatory
