01 — Diagnosis

Your site is powerful.
It just doesn't feel it.

YNOT.NOW has one of the most sophisticated multi-agent intelligence architectures in insurtech. But the experience hides it behind walls of text and static cards.

Current Pain Points
📄Text-heavy monotony — 27+ findings presented as identical card grids. No hierarchy of importance, no visual distinction between breakthrough signals and routine watches.
🧊Static, lifeless data — TRL scores, confidence ratings, trajectories — all encoded as tiny text. The data doesn't breathe or move or tell a story.
🔇Single modality — Pure text + cards. No audio, no visual narratives, no interactive exploration. Users scan, they don't discover.
⬜Generic aesthetic — White background, bordered cards, monospace labels. Competent but indistinguishable from any SaaS dashboard.
📱No engagement loops — No quizzes, flashcards, or ways to test understanding. Users consume passively rather than learn actively.
The Opportunity
🌌Immersive intelligence — Make every finding a destination. WebGL particle systems, scroll-driven narratives, spatial audio. Intelligence you can feel.
🎙️Multi-modal learning — Weekly AI-generated audio overviews (à la NotebookLM), flashcard decks, quizzes, and mind maps. Same data, five ways to absorb it.
🗺️Spatial data storytelling — Transform 27 findings into an interactive constellation. Zoom, filter, connect. See the landscape, not just the list.
🖤Luxury dark aesthetic — Rich blacks, gold accents, generous whitespace. Position YNOT.NOW as the premium intelligence source it actually is.
🧠Active engagement — Flashcards, quizzes, knowledge checks. Turn passive readers into informed professionals who retain what they learn.
02 — The Eight Concepts

Eight radical shifts.
Each one a first.

These aren't incremental improvements. Each concept pushes what a tech intelligence site can be — borrowing from immersive art, data journalism, and AI-native interfaces.

01
WebGL · Three.js
The Signal Constellation
High effort · High impact
Replace the card grid with an interactive 3D particle constellation. Each finding is a star — size = confidence, color = verdict, orbit = domain. Click to explore. Zoom to discover. The intelligence landscape becomes something you navigate, not just read.
→
The Vision

Imagine landing on YNOT.NOW and instead of a card grid, you see a living galaxy of intelligence. Each of the 27 weekly findings floats as a luminous particle in 3D space. Signal findings glow gold, Watch findings pulse white, Unverified ones dim at the edges. The bigger the particle, the higher the confidence. Clusters form naturally around domains — P&C stars orbit together, Life & Annuities form their own nebula. Click any star and it expands into a detailed finding card that slides in from the side. Drag to rotate the view. Scroll to zoom. This is NASA Eyes on Asteroids meets The Pudding meets Lusion — applied to insurance intelligence.

Key Features
🌟Particle physics — Each finding is a Three.js particle with mass (confidence), color (verdict), and orbital path (domain). They attract and repel based on topic similarity.
🔍Semantic zoom — Zooming in reveals finding titles, then full details. Like Google Maps but for intelligence signals.
🕸️Connection threads — When Weave (your systems thinker) identifies cross-domain connections, glowing threads appear between related findings.
⏱️Time scrubbing — Drag a timeline slider and watch the constellation evolve across weeks. See signals emerge, strengthen, and sometimes fade.
Tech Stack
Three.js r160+ Custom shaders (GLSL) OrbitControls Raycaster for click Canvas fallback for mobile
Inspiration

"The best data experiences don't just show you information — they make you feel like an explorer discovering it." — Inspired by Lusion's WebGL mastery, NASA Eyes on Asteroids, and The Pudding's data journalism.

02
AI Audio · Web Audio API
The Weekly Ear
Medium effort · Very high impact
Generate a weekly AI audio briefing — a 3-5 minute "podcast" where two AI voices discuss the top findings, debate what's signal vs. noise, and break down what insurance professionals should pay attention to. Think NotebookLM Audio Overviews, but for insurance AI intelligence.
→
The Vision

Every Monday after the cron runs, a new 3-5 minute audio briefing is auto-generated. Two distinct AI voices — one plays the optimist (like Scout), the other the skeptic (like Null) — and they have a natural-sounding conversation about the week's most important findings. The audio player sits as a persistent floating pill at the bottom of the screen with a waveform visualizer. Users can listen while reading, on their commute, or during their morning coffee. This makes YNOT.NOW the first insurance intelligence platform with a built-in AI podcast.

Key Features
🎙️Auto-generated weekly briefing — Claude generates a dialogue script from findings, then a TTS API (ElevenLabs/OpenAI) voices it with two distinct speakers.
📊Live waveform visualizer — Web Audio API analyzes the audio stream and renders a responsive waveform using Canvas, with peaks synced to emphasis words.
📝Timestamped transcript — Click any line in the transcript and the audio jumps to that moment. Finding cards highlight as they're discussed.
🔊Ambient data sonification — Optional: subtle background tones that shift based on the week's signal composition. More signals = warmer, more noise = cooler. A vibe, not noise.
Tech Stack
Anthropic API (script gen) ElevenLabs / OpenAI TTS Web Audio API Canvas waveform Supabase Storage
Inspiration

"NotebookLM proved that AI audio overviews aren't a gimmick — they're a new modality for understanding. YNOT.NOW could be the first vertical intelligence platform to adopt this."

03
Active Learning
Signal Flashcards & Quiz Mode
Medium effort · High impact
Auto-generate interactive flashcard decks and quizzes from each week's findings. "What TRL is parametric flood insurance at?" "Which mind flagged EU AI Act compliance risks?" Turn passive reading into active retention. A first for any intelligence platform.
→
The Vision

After browsing findings, users can tap "Test Your Knowledge" to enter Quiz Mode. Claude auto-generates 10 questions per week based on the findings — multiple choice, true/false, and "which mind said this?" format. There's also a Flashcard Deck mode: swipeable cards with the finding title on front, verdict + key insight on back. Streak tracking, weekly scores, and a subtle gamification layer make this addictive. Insurance professionals actually retain what they read instead of skimming and forgetting.

Key Features
🃏Swipeable flashcards — Tinder-style swipe UX. Swipe right = "I knew this", left = "Need to review". Spaced repetition algorithm resurfaces weak areas.
❓AI-generated quizzes — 10 questions per week, auto-generated from findings. Tracks accuracy over time. "Your insurance AI IQ: 87/100".
🔥Streak system — "You've checked in 4 weeks in a row." Lightweight gamification without being childish. Professional, not playful.
📊Knowledge gaps — "You're strong on P&C signals but haven't engaged with Climate & ESG findings." Personalized suggestions.
Tech Stack
Vanilla JS (gesture handling) CSS transforms (card flip) LocalStorage (scores) Anthropic API (quiz gen)
04
Interactive Data Viz
The Intelligence Mind Map
Medium effort · High impact
An auto-generated, interactive mind map showing how this week's findings connect across domains, agents, and themes. Nodes expand on click. Animated connections pulse between related signals. See the whole intelligence landscape at a glance.
→
The Vision

A force-directed graph that auto-organizes around themes, not just domains. The central node is the week. Branch out to domains (P&C, Life, Climate, etc.), then to individual findings, then to shared themes. When Scout and Terra both surface climate-related signals, a gold thread connects them. When Null challenges a finding, a red dashed line appears. The mind map is zoomable, pannable, and filterable — toggle by agent, verdict, or TRL level. This visualization makes Weave's cross-domain synthesis visible for the first time.

Tech Stack
D3.js force simulation SVG rendering Canvas (1000+ nodes) Zoom/pan controls
05
Scrollytelling
The Weekly Narrative
Medium effort · Very high impact
Transform the Weekly Pulse from a text block into a Rose Island-style scroll narrative. Each scroll step reveals a finding with cinematic transitions — parallax layers, animated data points, contextual imagery. The week's intelligence unfolds like a story, not a report.
→
The Vision

The default landing experience becomes a scroll-driven narrative. As the user scrolls, each major finding from the week slides into view with parallax-layered visuals — abstract generative art backgrounds, animated TRL gauges, confidence meters that fill in real-time. Between findings, contextual bridges connect the dots: "Meanwhile, on the regulatory front..." The whole thing feels like scrolling through a beautifully designed editorial feature in Bloomberg or The Information — but generated automatically from your cron output every week. Rose Island meets data journalism.

Key Features
📜IntersectionObserver-driven steps — Each finding triggers on scroll. Smooth fade-ins, slide-ups, and parallax layers create cinematic depth.
📈Animated data viz inline — TRL gauges animate from 0 to value. Confidence dots light up sequentially. Region tags fly in from map positions.
🎨Generative art backgrounds — Each domain gets a unique generative pattern (Canvas-drawn). P&C gets angular geometries, Life gets organic curves, Climate gets fluid gradients.
06
Auto-Generated
Weekly Infographic & Report
Medium effort · High impact
Auto-generate a shareable, beautifully designed infographic summarizing the week's intelligence. One-page visual showing signal distribution, top findings, TRL landscape, and regional breakdown. Downloadable as PNG/PDF. LinkedIn-ready.
→
The Vision

Every Monday, alongside the findings, YNOT.NOW auto-generates a publication-quality infographic. Think of it as an "Instagram Story for insurtech professionals." The infographic includes: a verdict donut chart (Signal/Watch/Unverified split), a TRL landscape bar showing where findings cluster on the readiness scale, top 3 headlines with verdict badges, a regional heat strip, and a Weave insight callout. The whole thing renders as a styled HTML page that can be screenshotted, downloaded as PNG, or exported as a one-page PDF. Users share it on LinkedIn, internal Slack channels, and board presentations.

Tech Stack
html2canvas (PNG export) jsPDF (PDF export) SVG charts Styled HTML template
07
Power User Mode
The Intelligence Table
Low-medium effort · High impact
A sortable, filterable, searchable data table mode for power users. Every finding across all weeks in one dense, Airtable-like view. Sort by TRL, filter by agent, search by keyword. Export to CSV. The analyst's dream interface.
→
The Vision

For researchers and analysts who want raw power over the data, the Intelligence Table offers an Airtable-like grid view of every finding ever collected. Columns: Title, Verdict, Agent, Domain, TRL, Confidence, Regions, Signal Status, Week, Refs. Click any column header to sort. Use the search bar to find "parametric" across all weeks. Filter by agent to see only what Null has challenged. Toggle between compact (one-line) and expanded (with body text) views. Export filtered results to CSV for further analysis. This is the B2B enterprise view that gives YNOT.NOW credibility with CTOs and analysts.

Key Features
🔍Full-text search — Instantly search across all finding titles and bodies. Highlight matching terms.
📊Multi-column sort — Sort by TRL descending, then by confidence. Keyboard-navigable for accessibility.
📥CSV/JSON export — Download filtered datasets for analysis in Excel, Python, or BI tools.
🔗Deep links — Each finding row has a permalink. Share a specific finding's URL directly.
08
Full Experience
The Dark Canvas — A New Homepage
High effort · Transformative
A complete visual identity overhaul. Dark luxury aesthetic with gold accents. Animated hero with generative art. Smooth page transitions. Ambient background particles. The homepage becomes an immersive art piece that happens to contain the world's best insurance AI intelligence.
→
The Vision

The entire YNOT.NOW experience shifts to a dark-on-dark luxury canvas. Think Lusion meets Bloomberg Terminal meets a high-end watch brand. The background is #050505 — near-black but warm. Gold (#c8a96e) replaces the monochrome accents. Generative particles drift across the background, subtly reacting to mouse movement. Page sections fade in with staggered animations as you scroll. Finding cards have depth — glassmorphism borders, subtle shadows, hover states that feel tactile. The typography stays DM Sans/Serif/Mono but the sizing becomes more dramatic — headlines at 120px+, body text with more breathing room. This isn't a redesign. It's a repositioning — from "useful dashboard" to "premium intelligence experience."

Key Features
🌑Dark luxury palette — #050505 background, #0c0c0c surfaces, #c8a96e gold accents. Warm blacks, not cold. Feels expensive.
✨Ambient particles — Subtle floating particles in the background, barely visible, that drift with a physics simulation. Reacts gently to mouse position.
🪟Glassmorphism cards — Finding cards have semi-transparent backgrounds with backdrop-filter blur. Borders glow on hover. Verdict badges pulse.
📐Dramatic typography — Hero text at 120-170px. Section headers at 60-80px. Generous letter-spacing. The type alone commands attention.
🎭View mode switcher — Persistent nav lets users switch between: Narrative (scrollytelling), Constellation (3D), Table (data), and Classic (cards). One data source, four experiences.
Inspiration

"Akari by Lusion shows that a website can feel like walking through an art gallery. Rose Island proves that storytelling trumps feature lists. YNOT.NOW should feel like opening a vault of premium intelligence — dark, quiet, powerful. The anti-dashboard."

The View Mode System

The cornerstone of this redesign is a persistent view mode switcher in the nav. Users choose how they want to consume the same intelligence:

📜Narrative Mode — The scroll-driven storytelling experience. Best for first-time visitors and weekly catch-ups. (Concept 5)
🌌Constellation Mode — The 3D WebGL exploration. Best for visual thinkers and presentations. (Concept 1)
📊Table Mode — The power-user data grid. Best for analysts and researchers. (Concept 7)
🃏Learn Mode — Flashcards, quizzes, and mind maps. Best for retention and team training. (Concept 3 + 4)
04 — Implementation Phases

Three phases.
Ship something radical every two weeks.

Each phase is independently valuable. Phase 1 alone transforms the experience. Each subsequent phase compounds the impact.

P1

Week 1-2 · Foundational
Full visual identity shift to dark luxury aesthetic. Animated hero with particles. Scroll-driven weekly narrative replaces the text-heavy Pulse. Infographic generation (PNG/PDF export). This alone makes YNOT.NOW feel like a completely different product.
P2
Multi-Modal Intelligence
Week 3-4 · Engagement
Audio briefing generation (The Weekly Ear). Flashcard and quiz system. Data table view for power users. Mind map visualization. The view mode switcher goes live. Users can now experience intelligence in five different ways.
P3
The Signal Constellation
Week 5-6 · Iconic
The full Three.js 3D constellation with semantic zoom, connection threads, and time scrubbing. This is the signature experience — the thing people screenshot and share. The thing that makes people say "I've never seen a tech site do this."
